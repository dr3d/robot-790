# NapEdge Logs

NapEdge runs are idle-rumination experiments where Eric is given a dense reference note and then left mostly alone. The goal is to see whether the idle loop can connect mechanisms, correct itself, use search, and return to conversation without collapsing into repetition or generic assistant behavior.

Raw captures stay in the project `logs/` tree. This folder is for public-safe excerpts and analysis packets.

## Curation Strategy

Keep three layers:

1. Raw local capture: complete conversation, events, audio, and media. This is evidence, not publication.
2. Curated excerpt file: enough timestamped material to show the behavior and its failure modes.
3. Public article: a short explanation of why the run matters, what was running, what worked, and what still needs work.

When curating a run, prefer excerpts that demonstrate behavior over excerpts that are merely pretty. Good tags are `SETUP`, `MECHANISM`, `LOOKUP`, `REVISION`, `SELF-MODEL`, `RETURN`, and `WATCH`.

For public material:

- Include date, model, settings, and source capture when known.
- Label Eric's claims as spoken claims unless they have been checked.
- Keep private personal details out unless Scott explicitly chooses to publish them.
- Preserve funny lines, but do not let charm hide a factual failure.
- Treat durable notes differently from spoken rumination. Spoken errors are cheap; written errors compound.

The house rule is simple: show the magic, show the machinery, and do not pretend the machinery is not there.
