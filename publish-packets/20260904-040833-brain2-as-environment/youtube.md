Title:
Robot 790: Brain2 As Environment

Description:
This run is a quieter one, but it catches an important design turn for Robot 790 / Eric.

Brain2 is not treated here as another executive brain that secretly controls the main voice. It works more like part of the room: mouth text, monitor voice, private observations, and a little friction. Scott can notice those side-channel thoughts and choose what to bring into the spoken conversation.

That makes the architecture feel less like one big hidden mind and more like a local companion system with lanes: one public mouth, one watcher, one human bridge, and receipts when the story gets weird.
