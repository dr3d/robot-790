For people who prefer to listen: a 22-minute companion to "Time And Space,
Both Live."

The article asks why Robot 790 can feel more occupied than a one-off generated
image or a fixed video without making a grand claim about hidden consciousness.
The answer is practical: a sampled character kept moving through real time,
a visible body, tools, notes, receipts, a face that can be wrong, and a shared
room has conditions to orient around.

This audio piece is an interpretation of the article, not a live run or new
evidence. The full text is on the project page.
