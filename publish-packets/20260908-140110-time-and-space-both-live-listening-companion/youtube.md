Description:
For people who prefer to listen, this is a 22-minute companion to the article
"Time And Space, Both Live."

It is an interpretation of the essay, not a live Eric run, a transcript, or
new evidence.

The article makes a deliberately modest argument: Robot 790 becomes more
legible when a sampled character is kept going through real time and an
inhabited physical situation. A face, body, tools, notes, receipts, loop,
maker, and shared room do not prove a hidden person in a machine. They give a
character conditions to orient around, make mistakes in, and repair within.

The useful frame is neither "just a chatbot" nor a consciousness claim. It is
a live, inspectable robot experiment whose mechanism stays visible.
